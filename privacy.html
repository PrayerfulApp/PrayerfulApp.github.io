Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

No Data Collection

Prayerful does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. All information entered in the app — including your name, prayer preferences, and app selections — is stored locally on your device and never leaves it.

No Servers or Accounts

We have no servers, no user accounts, and no analytics. Your data belongs to you and stays on your device.

Your Privacy is Complete

We do not use third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or tracking of any kind.
