Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

Overview

Prayerful is built to keep your prayer life private. Everything you enter in the app, including your name, prayers, prayer history, streak, and the apps you choose to lock, is stored only on your device. We have no servers, no user accounts and no analytics, and we never see your prayers or how you use your phone.

Information Stored on Your Device

Your name, prayers, prayer times, reading preferences, prayer history, and app selections stay on your device. They are not sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes this information.

Supporter Subscriptions

The optional Supporter subscription is sold and processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. Prayerful checks whether your subscription is active using Apple's StoreKit on your device. We do not run any servers or use any third-party subscription service, so we never receive your purchase history, your Apple Account, or your payment details.

Apple's handling of App Store purchases is described in Apple's privacy policy. You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time in Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions.

Information We Do Not Collect

Prayerful does not collect your name, email address, contacts, location, prayers, or prayer activity. We do not use advertising, analytics, or tracking services, and we do not sell or share data with advertisers or data brokers.

Device Permissions

Prayerful uses Apple's Screen Time API to lock the apps you choose. This permission is handled entirely by iOS on your device. Prayerful cannot see which apps you lock or how you use them, and no Screen Time information ever leaves your device.

If you turn on reminders, notifications are scheduled locally on your device.

Children's Privacy

Prayerful does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Purchases on a child's device are controlled by Apple's Family Sharing and Ask to Buy settings.

Changes to This Policy

If we update this privacy policy, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

Contact

For questions, contact us at prayerful.app@gmail.com.
